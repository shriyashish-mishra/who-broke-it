# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a friendly, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity. This is a short summary in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which we adopt by reference.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; assume good faith; give and accept feedback graciously; focus on what is best for the project and its users; credit others' work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal or political attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or attention, sustained disruption of discussions, or publishing others' private information.

The humor in this project is aimed at bugs and broken builds, never at people. "Who broke it?" is a question about commits, not a license to blame a teammate.

**Scope:** applies in project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, chat) and when representing the project publicly.

**Enforcement:** report concerns to the maintainers (see the repository's contact or security policy address). Maintainers will review reports promptly and privately, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants. Reporters' privacy will be respected.
